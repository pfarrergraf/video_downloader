# Concept 5 — "Three Free a Day, No Sign-Up" (lead candidate)

**Format:** 20s vertical (9:16) · **Tone:** spy-movie parody · **Platform:** Reels/Shorts/TikTok

## Logline
Someone treats "not giving out their personal info" like defusing a bomb in a
heist movie. Punchline: the product genuinely doesn't ask for any.

## Dialogue / VO
- Muttered "mission briefing" line at 0:03: *"Objective: save video. Do NOT give up email."* (can be on-screen caption instead of spoken, actor's choice)
- Closing VO: **"Three free downloads a day. No account required. Mission complete."**

## Sound design notes
- Spy-parody music bed (staccato strings + single horn hit) should be played completely straight — the comedy comes from the mismatch between the stakes (mock-heroic) and the actual task (downloading a video), not from a jokey/silly score.
- Alarm blare in shot 3 is the only "cheap" sound gag — keep everything else deadpan-serious in execution.

## Why this one's safe
This is the safest concept content-wise: "3 free downloads per 24h, no account" is a literal, current product fact per the guardrails doc — no exaggeration needed.

## Compliance check
- Confirm "3 downloads per 24 hours without an account" is still accurate at time of publishing (guardrails doc already lists this as current policy — re-verify against code/settings before shipping, not just this doc).
- The generic "sign-up form" in shot 3 must be an original mockup, not a real competitor's form/branding.
- "Mission complete" / spy framing is fine — no real government/agency insignia, trademarks, or movie title references.
