# Concept 3 — "The Serial Re-Downloader"

**Format:** 22s vertical (9:16) · **Tone:** Groundhog-Day deadpan · **Platform:** Reels/Shorts/TikTok

## Logline
Same person loses the same video three times in three different dumb ways. The
fourth time is anticlimactic on purpose — that's the joke.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Action | VO / SFX |
|---|------|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Medium shot, bedroom | Person watching a video, phone dies mid-save. Black screen. Sigh. | On-screen caption: "Attempt 1" |
| 2 | 0:05–0:10 | Same setup, different outfit (implies days later) | Finds the video again, clears "cache" in settings out of habit, video's gone with it. Facepalm. | On-screen caption: "Attempt 2" |
| 3 | 0:10–0:15 | Same setup, third outfit | Finds it a third time, says "I'll save it later," visibly forgets, credits-style time-skip transition | On-screen caption: "Attempt 3" |
| 4 | 0:15–0:19 | Fourth time, calm | Finds it again, no drama this time — pastes link into DownloadThat!, taps once, looks at camera unimpressed | On-screen caption: "Attempt 4" |
| 5 | 0:19–0:22 | Screen recording | File already sitting in Downloads. Logo + CTA | VO (bored, matter-of-fact): "Yeah. That's it." |

## Dialogue / VO
- Optional single spoken line at the very end (attempt 3): *"I'll just save it later."* (delivered with obvious doom)
- Closing VO: **"Yeah. That's it."** — deliberately undersold, deadpan.

## Sound design notes
- Each "attempt" caption card gets a small comedic stamp/thud SFX (like a game-show wrong-answer buzzer, but softer/funnier, not harsh).
- Time-skip transition in shot 3 = quick whip-pan or film-leader flicker, with a short "whoosh."
- Final shot has almost no sound design — the anticlimax is the point, don't score it dramatically.

## Compliance check
- The video-within-the-video (what the person is trying to save) must be original/generic content — no real platform UI, no implying it was scraped from a specific named site.
- Don't frame "clearing cache" or "phone dying" as reasons the *competitor* app failed — frame it as ordinary bad luck, not a competitive claim.

## Asset needs
- 1 actor, 4 outfit/lighting variations (or AI-generated character consistency across 4 shots — check refs/ for a consistent character reference before generating)
- 3 caption-card motion graphics ("Attempt 1/2/3/4")
- Real screen recording of DownloadThat!'s Downloads folder view for shot 5
