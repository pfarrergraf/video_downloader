# Concept 2 — "Notification Hell" (lead candidate)

**Format:** 18s vertical (9:16) · **Tone:** horror-parody jump-scare · **Platform:** Reels/Shorts/TikTok

## Logline
Saving a video through a generic bloated app is a jump-scare gauntlet of popups.
DownloadThat! is dead silent by comparison — and that silence is the punchline.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Action | VO / SFX |
|---|------|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:02 | POV, thumb hovering over a generic "Download" button | Tension chord starts (horror-trailer style) | SFX: low string sting |
| 2 | 0:02–0:04 | Full-screen popup slams in | "🔔 Allow notifications?" — actor recoils like it's a monster jump-scare | SFX: horror stinger hit, hard cut |

## Dialogue / VO
- No dialogue during the horror beats — visual + SFX carries it entirely.
- Single VO line at the end: **"No account. No ads. Just downloads."**

## Sound design notes
- This is the SFX-heaviest concept — lean on `higgsfield-generate`'s sound-effect generation for the horror stingers (3 escalating hits) and the tremolo rise in shot 5.
- The one full second of silence in shot 6 is load-bearing comedy — don't shorten it in editing.

## Compliance check
- The "generic bloated app" shown in shots 1–5 must be an original, unbranded mockup UI — no real competitor's logo, color scheme, or exact layout copied.
- "No ads" and "No account" are both literally true per the product's current feature set — confirm this is still accurate before publishing (SDK/tracking claims are conditional on code verification per the guardrails doc).
- Don't frame this as "unlike [competitor name]" — keep it generic, no named comparison.

## Asset needs
- 1 mockup "bloated app" UI (motion graphic, unbranded) with 3 popup variants
- 3 horror-stinger SFX + 1 tremolo rise (AI-generated via sound-effects skill)
- Real screen recording of DownloadThat!'s actual queue flow for shot 7
