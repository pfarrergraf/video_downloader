# Concept 1 — "The Group Chat Save"

**Format:** 20s vertical (9:16) · **Tone:** chaos vs. calm · **Platform:** Reels/Shorts/TikTok

## Logline
Four people panic-scramble to save a video before it vanishes from a group chat.
One person doesn't panic.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Action | VO / SFX |
|---|------|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Close-up, phone screen | Group chat notification: "this vid is getting deleted in 1hr 😭" | SFX: notification ping, ticking clock starts under everything |
| 2 | 0:03–0:06 | Quick cut, person A | Frantically screen-recording their own screen while the video plays | SFX: panicked breathing (comedic, exaggerated) |
| 3 | 0:06–0:09 | Quick cut, person B | Re-forwarding the video to 6 other chats "just in case" | SFX: whoosh x6, rapid-fire |
| 4 | 0:09–0:12 | Quick cut, person C | AirDropping to their own laptop, missing, trying again | SFX: comedic "bloop" fail sound |
| 5 | 0:12–0:15 | Quick cut, person D | Taking a photo of their screen with ANOTHER phone | SFX: exaggerated camera shutter |
| 6 | 0:15–0:18 | Wide/calm, person E | Sitting still, pastes the link into DownloadThat!'s queue screen, taps once | Ticking clock SFX stops abruptly. Silence. |
| 7 | 0:18–0:20 | Screen recording, app UI | File sitting in Downloads, done. Logo + one-line CTA | VO (dry, deadpan): "Or you could just save it." |

## Dialogue / VO
- Only one VO line, at the very end: **"Or you could just save it."**
- Everything before it is silent-comedy / reaction shots — no dialogue needed, the ticking clock + reactions carry it.

## Sound design notes
- Ticking clock is the comedic spine — starts loud at 0:00, gets more frantic/pitched-up through shots 2–5, cuts dead silent at shot 6. That silence *is* the joke.
- No music track needed under the chaos beats — diegetic panic SFX only. A single calm ambient tone can start at shot 6.

## Compliance check (against `docs/MARKETING_LEGAL_GUARDRAILS.md`)
- Video shown must be generic/placeholder content the "sender" made themselves, not a recognizable copyrighted clip or platform UI.
- Don't show a real messaging app's logo/UI in shots 1–5 — use a generic chat bubble UI.
- No claim that content "disappears" from any real platform by name — this is a hypothetical framing device, keep it platform-agnostic.

## Asset needs
- 5 short live-action or AI-generated reaction clips (or one actor, 5 wardrobe/setting changes)
- 1 generic chat-bubble UI overlay (motion graphic, not a real app's chrome)
- Final screen recording of the actual DownloadThat! queue UI (real screencap, not AI-generated)
