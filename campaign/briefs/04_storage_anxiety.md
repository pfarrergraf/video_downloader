# Concept 4 — "Storage Anxiety, Dramatized"

**Format:** 18s vertical (9:16) · **Tone:** mock-horror melodrama · **Platform:** Reels/Shorts/TikTok

**⚠ Needs the most compliance care of the five — see checklist below before
using any line from this brief as final VO.**

## Logline
A "storage almost full" warning gets the horror-movie bomb-countdown treatment.
Payoff is relief, not an offline/no-network claim.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Action | VO / SFX |
|---|------|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Extreme close-up, phone screen | "Storage almost full" banner, storage bar at 97% | SFX: low ominous drone starts |
| 2 | 0:03–0:06 | Zoom push-in on the storage bar like a bomb timer | Actor's face in soft-focus background, horrified | SFX: heartbeat thump, rising tempo |
| 3 | 0:06–0:09 | Quick cuts: actor deleting photos, deleting apps, in a cold sweat | Comedic overreaction — sweating, panicked breathing | SFX: heartbeat peaks, glass-shatter stinger |
| 4 | 0:09–0:10 | Hard cut, black | — | Silence |
| 5 | 0:10–0:15 | Calm reveal, screen recording | DownloadThat! queue UI, download completing, no bloated companion download, no separate "cloud sync" step shown | Ambient calm pad fades in |
| 6 | 0:15–0:18 | Logo + CTA card | — | VO (relieved, warm): "Lighter on your phone. Not on your nerves." |

## Dialogue / VO
- No dialogue during panic beats.
- Closing VO: **"Lighter on your phone. Not on your nerves."**

## Sound design notes
- Heartbeat + drone combo carries the tension; glass-shatter stinger at the peak (shot 3→4 cut) is the release valve before the hard silence.
- Keep the calm pad in shot 5 genuinely calm — no swelling "epic" music, undercuts the joke.

## Compliance check — read before finalizing VO
- **Do not** say or imply "fully offline," "no internet needed," or "no cloud/network at all" — licensing checks, Google Play Billing, and updates use limited network services per `docs/MARKETING_LEGAL_GUARDRAILS.md`. "Lighter on your phone" (storage/footprint claim) is fine; a no-network claim is not.
- Avoid absolute language like "never runs out of space" — frame as "media processing happens on your device," not a storage-capacity promise.
- Do not name or visually reference a specific competitor's file size or install size — keep the "bloat" being satirized generic.

- Bomb-timer-style storage-bar motion graphic
- Real screen recording of DownloadThat!'s download-complete flow for shot 5
- Heartbeat + drone + glass-shatter SFX (AI-generated)
