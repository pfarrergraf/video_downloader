# Concept 6 — "Wait, You Don't Need An Account For That"

Generated via the `jackyshen-gen-short-video-script` skill (Tool Demo /
Golden Structure), deliberately different structure from concepts 1-5: direct-
to-camera talking head + on-screen text overlays + real screen-recorded tool
demo, not a sketch/scene. **Format:** 45s vertical (9:16) · **Platform:**
TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

## Script

### [0-5s] Hook — Contrarian
**Visual:** Close-up, direct eye contact, phone held up like showing a friend.
**Line:** "You think you need to make an account just to save a video? You don't. Watch."

### [5-10s] Pain point
**Visual:** Quick cut, exasperated expression, on-screen text overlay: "Email. Password. 'Verify your account.' For... saving a video."
**Line:** "Every app wants your whole life story before you can download one clip."

### [10-15s] Value promise
**Visual:** Hold phone toward camera.
**Line:** "This one just wants the link. That's it."

### [15-20s] Confidence / lower the barrier
**Visual:** On-screen text overlay: "Takes about 10 seconds."
**Line:** "No tutorial needed. Paste, tap, done."

### [20-45s] Tutorial — real screen recording (tool intro + actionable steps)
**Visual:** Real screen recording of DownloadThat!, not AI-generated — paste
a link → queue shows progress → file lands in Downloads. On-screen text
overlays label each step ("1. Paste link" / "2. Tap download" / "3. It's
already there").
**Line (voiceover over the recording):** "Paste your link. Tap download.
Check your downloads folder — it's already there. Three free ones a day, no
account. If you need more, Pro's a one-time purchase — not another
subscription you'll forget to cancel."

### [45s] Close / CTA
**Visual:** Back to talking head, warm smile.
**Line:** "That's genuinely it. Link in bio."

## Production notes
- **Camera:** Phone, front-facing, handheld-casual (not tripod-locked) — matches the "showing a friend" framing device
- **Lighting:** Natural/bright, not the moody spy-parody grade used in concepts 1-5 — this one should look like a real person filming themselves, deliberately different visual register
- **Background:** Simple, plain room — no set dressing needed
- **Music:** None or very low, mostly voice-driven
- **Captions:** Required, burned-in (accessibility + platform norm)
- **The 20-45s block is the one segment that requires real footage** — this is the actual app being used, not a generated mockup. Everything else could be shot on a phone in one take.

## Compliance check
- "No account" / "3 free a day" / "Pro is one-time purchase, not a subscription" are all current, verified product facts per `docs/MARKETING_LEGAL_GUARDRAILS.md` — no exaggeration needed.
- Does not claim the app works on "any" site/platform — stays generic ("your link").
- No anti-app-store framing; doesn't mention sideloading at all.
- No fabricated stats, no manufactured urgency.

## Why this structure, deliberately
Concepts 1-5 are all sketch/scene comedy with a generated character. This one
is the "tool demo" archetype instead — direct address, real screen capture,
contrarian hook, low production overhead. It's the cheapest concept to
actually shoot for real (one phone, one take, one screen recording) and it's
the only one that doesn't depend on AI-generated visuals holding up under
scrutiny — worth having at least one concept that isn't gated on generation
quality.
