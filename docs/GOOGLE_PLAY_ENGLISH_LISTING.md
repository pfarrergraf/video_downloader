# Google Play: English store listing

Prepared for the English translation of the default Google Play store listing.
Use this file with the Claude Chrome extension while the Play Console is open.

## Fields to enter

### App name

```text
DownloadThat!
```

### Short description

```text
Download video and audio from links you are allowed to save.
```

This is within Google Play's 80-character limit.

### Full description

```text
DownloadThat saves video and audio from links you are legally allowed to save — directly on your device.

KEY FEATURES
• One field for a link, multiple links, or an entire playlist
• Download video or audio (MP3) separately
• Quality selection from 240p up to 4K
• Automatic progress bar for active downloads
• Choose your own download folder, open or share files directly
• Runs on your device — no ads, no tracking
• Available in many languages

FREE TO USE
The free version offers full quality up to 4K, limited by a daily download quota. DownloadThat Pro removes this limit.

IMPORTANT NOTICE
DownloadThat is a technical tool. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights to the content. The app does not bypass DRM or paywalls. Please download only your own content, public-domain or appropriately licensed content, or content you have explicit permission to download.
```

The full description is within Google Play's 4,000-character limit.

## Graphics and screenshots

Use the same graphics as the default German listing unless localized graphics are
available. Google Play allows the default-language graphics to be reused for an
English translation.

- App icon: `C:\ai\video_downloader\store_assets\icon-512.png`
- Feature graphic: `C:\ai\video_downloader\store_assets\feature_graphic-1024x500.png`
- Phone screenshots:
  - `C:\ai\video_downloader\store_assets\screenshot_main.png`
  - `C:\ai\video_downloader\store_assets\screenshot_queue.png`
  - `C:\ai\video_downloader\store_assets\screenshot_settings.png`

Do not add a YouTube video URL. Leave the video fields empty.
Leave tablet, Chromebook and Android XR screenshot sections empty unless
matching real screenshots are available.

## Safe Claude Chrome procedure

1. Open the English translation in `Manage translations`.
2. Enter only the three text fields above.
3. Reuse the existing icon, feature graphic and phone screenshots if Play
   Console asks for localized graphics.
4. Leave video and unsupported-device sections unchanged.
5. Save the English listing.
6. Stop before `Submit for review` and show the resulting preview/status.

Do not change the package name, app signing settings, payment settings, testers,
or release tracks while editing this listing.
